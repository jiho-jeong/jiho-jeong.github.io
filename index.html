About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Hydro Science and Engineering Research at the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology (KICT). I received my Ph.D. in Geology from Kyungpook National University (2025), where my dissertation focused on improving the real-world applicability of data-driven hydrogeological models through physics-informed approaches.

My work sits at the boundary between hydrology and machine learning: embedding governing equations into recurrent networks, transferring knowledge from data-rich to data-scarce monitoring wells, and using generative models to disentangle climatic noise from aquifer signals. At KICT I now extend these ideas to river-flood and urban-inundation forecasting, and to LLM-based decision support for flood warning operations.

At a glance

  • Physics-informed neural networks (PINN) for groundwater level dynamics
  • Transfer learning & multi-station modeling under data scarcity
  • Generative models (DAE / CVAE) for hydraulic property estimation
  • AI–physics hybrid flood forecasting & urban-flood surrogate models
  • Graph neural networks for spatiotemporal hydrology
  • LLM-based decision support for flood-warning operations
